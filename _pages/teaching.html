Topics covered include the nonlinear susceptibility and its formal properties, second- and third-order classical nonlinear optics, quantization of the electromagnetic field in nonlinear media, quantum states of light, sub-shot-noise interferometry, and the quantum theory of parametric amplifiers, parametric oscillators, and squeezing via the Kerr effect. - Nonlinear response - Effects arising from second-order nonlinearity - Effects arising from third-order nonlinearity - Quantum states of light - Quantum nonlinear optics - Combined notes ### AEP 4230/5230 Statistical Physics An upper-level undergraduate course on statistical physics, covering thermodynamics, probability, classical and quantum statistical mechanics, interacting systems, and Langevin dynamics. In 2025 we used Kardar's *Statistical Physics of Particles* as the text for most of the course.
